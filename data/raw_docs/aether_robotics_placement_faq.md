# Aether Robotics — Engineering Campus Placement FAQ

## Who is Aether Robotics?

Aether Robotics is an autonomous-systems startup founded in 2019 and headquartered in
Bengaluru. The company designs and manufactures warehouse automation robots and the
accompanying fleet-management software. With 350 employees and Series B funding of
$48 million (2023), Aether is one of India's fastest-growing deep-tech employers.
Engineering is split across hardware (mechanical and electronics), embedded systems,
perception and planning algorithms, and cloud infrastructure.

## Which roles does Aether recruit for on campus?

Aether runs campus drives for three software-adjacent roles:

### Embedded Software Engineer (ESE)
Works on firmware for robot controllers, sensor interfaces (LiDAR, IMU, encoders), and
real-time operating systems (RTOS). C and C++ are the primary languages; knowledge of
ROS 2 is a strong plus.

**CTC**: ₹13 LPA.

### Perception & Planning Engineer (PPE)
Develops algorithms for simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), obstacle avoidance,
and motion planning. Requires a solid foundation in linear algebra, probability, and
signal processing. Python and C++ are used.

**CTC**: ₹14 LPA.

### Cloud & Fleet Software Engineer (CFSE)
Builds the backend services that coordinate robot fleets, handle telemetry ingestion, and
expose APIs to warehouse management systems. Stack includes Go, PostgreSQL, Kafka, and
Kubernetes.

**CTC**: ₹12 LPA.

## Eligibility

- **Eligible branches**: CSE, ECE, EEE, Mechanical Engineering, and Mathematics &
  Computing. Candidates from other branches are not eligible.
- **CGPA**: 7.0 minimum. No relaxation.
- **Backlogs**: Zero active backlogs. Prior cleared backlogs require a written explanation
  but do not disqualify.
- **Degree level**: Aether hires both B.Tech and M.Tech/M.E. candidates. M.Tech candidates
  are assessed for the PPE role first; if not selected, they may be considered for other
  roles with their consent.
- **Research publications**: Not required, but a peer-reviewed publication or strong thesis
  work in robotics, control systems, or computer vision significantly strengthens a
  candidacy for the PPE role.

## Interview Process

Aether's campus recruitment involves three rounds:

### Technical Assessment (60 minutes, online)
- Section A (30 minutes): 20 MCQs on operating systems, computer networks, embedded
  systems fundamentals, and C/C++ programming concepts.
- Section B (30 minutes): One algorithm problem (arrays or graph traversal) and one
  maths/linear-algebra problem (matrix operations or probability).

No negative marking. Top 25 candidates (per college, across all three roles) proceed.

### Technical Interview (60–90 minutes, in person or video)
Two interviewers (one domain-specific, one generalist). Structure varies by role:

**For ESE candidates**:
- C/C++ memory management, interrupt handling, mutex and semaphore concepts.
- One debugging exercise: a piece of C code with a logical or concurrency bug is shared;
  candidate must identify and fix it.

**For PPE candidates**:
- Derivation or explanation of a core SLAM or Kalman filter concept.
- Discussion of a candidate's computer-vision or robotics project.
- One mathematical problem: may involve probability, Bayesian inference, or matrix
  decomposition.

**For CFSE candidates**:
- Distributed systems fundamentals: CAP theorem, eventual consistency.
- One Go or Python coding problem (candidates may choose language).
- Database design: schema for a telemetry ingestion system.

### HR and Culture Interview (30 minutes)
Aether's founders and senior leadership place strong emphasis on mission alignment —
candidates are asked to articulate why they want to work in robotics and autonomous
systems, not just software engineering broadly. Candidates who cannot connect their
interests to the domain are rarely selected regardless of technical performance.

## Compensation and Perks

- ESOPs are offered to all full-time hires (0.01–0.05 % of post-money equity depending
  on role and negotiation, vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff).
- Joining bonus of ₹50,000 for candidates who accept within 7 days of offer.
- Flexible work: Bengaluru HQ, hybrid after the 6-month probation period.
- Hardware budget: ₹20,000 annual allowance for development boards, tools, or books.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I apply to multiple roles in the same drive?**
A: Yes. Indicate your preference order on the application form. You will be assessed
for your top-preference role first; if not selected, Aether may consider you for your
second preference with your consent.

**Q: Does Aether sponsor M.Tech students for their thesis research?**
A: Aether offers a research internship programme for pre-final-year M.Tech students
(separate from the campus placement drive). Successfully completing the internship
creates a direct-to-offer pathway for the PPE role.

**Q: Is relocation support provided?**
A: Yes. Aether provides a ₹30,000 one-time relocation allowance for candidates joining
from outside Bengaluru.
