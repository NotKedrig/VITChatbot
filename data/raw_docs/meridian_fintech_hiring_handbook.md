# Meridian Financial Technologies — Campus Hiring Handbook

## About Meridian FinTech

Meridian Financial Technologies (MFT) is a fintech startup founded in 2017, now employing
over 600 people across its offices in Mumbai and Chennai. MFT operates a lending
platform serving small and medium businesses and a B2C mobile payments product with
12 million active users. The engineering team is polyglot but leans heavily on Java
and Kotlin for backend services and React Native for mobile.

## Eligibility Requirements

MFT targets candidates from a specific academic profile:

- **Branches**: Computer Science, Information Technology, and Mathematics & Computing.
  Other branches are not considered for engineering roles.
- **CGPA**: Minimum 7.5 on a 10-point scale. For candidates from top-25 NIRF-ranked
  institutions, the threshold is relaxed to 7.0.
- **Backlogs**: No active backlogs. No history of more than two backlogs across the
  entire programme.
- **Internship**: A prior internship (paid or unpaid, minimum 4 weeks) is strongly
  preferred for the Software Development Engineer role but is not mandatory.

MFT does not recruit from MCA or MSc programmes for engineering roles.

## Roles

### Software Development Engineer (SDE-1)
Full-time engineering role contributing to backend microservices, payment APIs, or
mobile app features.

**Total CTC**: ₹12 LPA (₹10 LPA fixed + ₹2 LPA variable, performance-linked).

### Associate Data Scientist
Works within the risk analytics team building credit-scoring models, fraud-detection
pipelines, and customer-churn models. Requires strong command of Python, probability,
and statistics.

**Total CTC**: ₹11 LPA (₹9 LPA fixed + ₹2 LPA variable).

### Quality Assurance Engineer (QAE)
Responsible for test automation using Selenium and Appium, API testing, and performance
testing. Background in programming is required — manual-testing-only profiles are not
considered.

**Total CTC**: ₹8 LPA (fixed).

## Hiring Process

### Round 1 — Coding Assessment (Online, 75 minutes)
Platform: Codility. Three coding problems:
- Problem 1: Easy — string manipulation or array operations.
- Problem 2: Medium — dynamic programming or tree traversal.
- Problem 3: Hard (partial credit awarded) — graph algorithms or advanced data structures.

Candidates scoring above the 60th percentile within the session advance to Round 2.

### Round 2 — Technical Phone Screen (45 minutes, video call)
One interviewer (engineering manager or senior SDE). Format:
- 20 minutes: one coding problem shared on a collaborative editor; verbal explanation
  of thought process is weighted equally to correctness.
- 15 minutes: discussion of candidate's resume, projects, and a few CS fundamentals
  (time/space complexity, REST API design, database basics).
- 10 minutes: candidate's questions for MFT.

Feedback is provided within 3 business days. Approximately 40 % of candidates who
reach this round advance.

### Round 3 — Technical Panel Interview (90 minutes, on-site or video)
Three interviewers across two back-to-back sessions:

**Session A (45 minutes)**
- Deep-dive on one of the candidate's projects: architecture decisions, trade-offs,
  and what the candidate would change with hindsight.
- Two algorithm problems; the second typically requires optimization of the first
  solution.

**Session B (45 minutes)**
- System design: design a simplified payment processing system or a notification
  delivery service. Candidates are assessed on identifying bottlenecks, choosing
  appropriate data stores, and discussing consistency vs. availability trade-offs.
- Culture-fit questions aligned to MFT's values: customer obsession, speed of
  execution, and frugal innovation.

### Round 4 — HR and Offer Discussion (30 minutes)
Compensation, joining date (MFT requires joining within 90 days of offer), ESOP
eligibility (vesting cliff at 12 months), and relocation support (₹25,000 one-time
allowance for candidates relocating to Mumbai or Chennai).

## Additional Information

- MFT does not hold repeat drives; candidates who are not selected must wait until
  the next annual cycle.
- Background verification covers education credentials, employment history (if any),
  and criminal record; offer is conditional on successful verification.
- MFT's probation period is 6 months. Confirmation requires a satisfactory mid-probation
  review.
