# NovaTech Solutions — Offer Letter and Onboarding FAQ

## After Receiving an Offer

Candidates who clear all four rounds of NovaTech's campus drive receive a verbal
confirmation from the HR representative before leaving the venue. The formal offer
letter is sent to the candidate's registered email address within 10 business days
of the drive date.

### Offer Letter Contents

The NovaTech offer letter includes:
- Full name and permanent address of the candidate.
- Role title and grade (SE-P Grade 1, SE-Plat Grade 1, or GTD).
- Reporting location (Pune or Hyderabad) and expected start date.
- CTC breakup: fixed component, variable target, and applicable allowances
  (house rent allowance, conveyance allowance, and special allowance).
- Joining deadline: candidates must accept the offer digitally (via NovaTech's HR
  portal) within 15 calendar days of the offer letter date.
- Documents required on Day 1 (listed below).

### Offer Acceptance

Acceptance is completed on NovaTech's candidate portal (link provided in the offer
email). Required steps:
1. Log in using your registered email and the one-time password sent separately.
2. Review the offer letter PDF.
3. Upload acceptance form (signed) or accept electronically using Aadhaar-based eSign.
4. Confirm your preferred joining date (within the window specified in the offer).

**Note**: Not accepting within 15 days is treated as rejection of the offer. No
extensions are granted except in cases of documented medical emergency.

## Pre-Joining Checklist

Between offer acceptance and the Day 1 joining date, candidates must complete:

- **Background verification consent**: NovaTech uses an external BGV vendor (SpringVerify).
  A consent link is sent within 5 days of offer acceptance. The BGV covers academic
  credentials, any prior employment (internship letters count), and criminal record.
  Offers are conditional on successful BGV.
- **Medical fitness form**: A one-page self-declaration of medical fitness is required.
  Candidates with a declared health condition are not automatically disqualified; NovaTech
  provides reasonable accommodation per its policy.
- **UAN and PF nomination**: Candidates must provide their Universal Account Number
  if they have one, or consent to new PF account creation.
- **Bank account details**: NovaTech disburses salaries to a specific partner bank
  (HDFC Bank); new accounts are opened on Day 1 if the candidate does not already
  have one.

## Day 1 — Documents Required

Bring the following original documents (plus self-attested photocopies) on Day 1:

- All academic mark sheets and degree certificates (or provisional certificate if
  results are still awaited).
- Two government-issued photo ID proofs (Aadhaar card mandatory; PAN card, passport,
  or driving licence as second ID).
- Three passport-size photographs.
- Experience or internship letters (if any).
- Original offer letter printout.

Failure to produce originals on Day 1 may delay or invalidate onboarding.

## Onboarding Programme

NovaTech's induction programme spans the first four weeks:

### Week 1 — Company and Culture Induction
- Meet-and-greet with senior leadership (virtual town hall).
- Overview of NovaTech's products, business model, and engineering organisation.
- Compliance training: code of conduct, data-privacy policy, anti-harassment guidelines,
  and information-security orientation.
- IT setup: laptop provisioning, access to internal tools, VPN configuration.

### Week 2 — Engineering Bootcamp
- Hands-on workshops on NovaTech's primary technology stack: Spring Boot, React,
  PostgreSQL, and the internal CI/CD toolchain.
- Architecture deep dive: how NovaTech's microservices communicate, service-mesh basics,
  and the monitoring stack (Prometheus, Grafana, and PagerDuty).
- Introduction to the engineering wiki and contribution guidelines.

### Week 3 — Team Integration
- Move to assigned squad. First task: set up local development environment and run
  the application end-to-end locally.
- Shadow a senior engineer for 3–5 days.
- Pick up a first bug-fix ticket labelled "good-first-issue" in the squad's backlog.

### Week 4 — First Deliverable
- Complete and merge the first pull request (feature or bug fix).
- Participate in the sprint retrospective and planning ceremony.
- 30-day check-in meeting with the reporting manager.

## Probation Period

All NovaTech campus hires are on a 6-month probation. During probation:
- Leave entitlement is limited to 6 days (plus public holidays).
- Notice period is 1 month.
- At the end of month 5, the reporting manager conducts a formal probation review;
  successful completion converts to permanent employment with a 3-month notice period
  and full leave entitlement (18 days per year).
