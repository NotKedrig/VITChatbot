# NovaTech Solutions — Campus Recruitment Guide

## Company Overview

NovaTech Solutions is a mid-sized software product company headquartered in Pune, India,
with engineering offices in Hyderabad and Bengaluru. Founded in 2010, NovaTech builds
cloud-based SaaS platforms for supply-chain analytics and logistics optimization. The
engineering workforce of approximately 1,800 employees includes software engineers,
data scientists, DevOps specialists, and product managers. NovaTech is known for its
engineering-first culture and invests heavily in internal developer tooling.

## Eligibility Criteria

NovaTech hires exclusively from on-campus drives for entry-level roles. The following
academic criteria apply for all undergraduate engineering candidates:

- **Degree**: B.E. / B.Tech in Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics and
  Communication, or Electrical Engineering.
- **Minimum CGPA**: 7.0 on a 10-point scale (no rounding).
- **Active backlogs**: Zero active backlogs at the time of the drive. Historical backlogs
  that have been cleared are acceptable.
- **Year of passing**: Current final-year students or recent graduates (within one year of
  graduation).
- **Batch gap**: Candidates with a year gap during undergraduate study must submit a written
  explanation; a gap does not automatically disqualify.

Candidates from MCA programmes are also eligible for Software Engineer roles if they meet
the CGPA threshold.

## Roles Offered

### Software Engineer — Product (SE-P)
Entry-level individual-contributor role on one of NovaTech's product squads.
Responsibilities include feature development, code reviews, writing unit and integration
tests, and participating in sprint ceremonies.

**CTC**: ₹8.5 LPA (fixed) + ₹1.0 LPA performance bonus target (Year 1).

### Software Engineer — Platform (SE-Plat)
Focused on internal developer tooling, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud infrastructure.
Candidates with a demonstrated interest in DevOps or systems programming are preferred.

**CTC**: ₹9.0 LPA (fixed) + ₹1.0 LPA performance bonus target (Year 1).

### Graduate Trainee — Data (GTD)
12-month structured trainee programme for candidates with strong mathematics and
statistics backgrounds. Trainees rotate across data engineering, ML model deployment,
and analytics engineering teams before being confirmed as Data Engineers or ML Engineers.

**Stipend during training**: ₹45,000 per month.
**Post-confirmation CTC**: ₹10.0 LPA.

## Recruitment Process

The NovaTech campus drive is conducted in a single day with the following stages in order:

### Stage 1 — Online Assessment (90 minutes)
Administered on HackerRank. Sections:
- Quantitative Aptitude: 20 questions, 25 minutes.
- Logical Reasoning: 15 questions, 20 minutes.
- Programming MCQ: 15 questions covering data structures, algorithms, OS concepts, DBMS,
  and OOP; 25 minutes.
- Coding: 2 problems (one easy, one medium by LeetCode classification), 20 minutes.

Cutoff scores are not published. NovaTech shortlists approximately the top 30 % of
candidates per college, subject to role-specific quotas.

### Stage 2 — Technical Interview Round 1 (45–60 minutes)
One-on-one with a senior engineer (SDE II or above). Focus areas:
- Data structures and algorithms: expect at least one live coding problem on arrays,
  strings, trees, or graphs.
- Object-oriented design: candidate may be asked to design a class hierarchy for a
  real-world scenario (e.g., a library management system or a parking-lot system).
- Project discussion: 10–15 minutes on the candidate's strongest project or internship.
  Interviewers look for depth of understanding, not just description.

### Stage 3 — Technical Interview Round 2 (30–45 minutes)
Panel of two interviewers (one from the hiring team, one from an adjacent team).
Focus areas:
- System design fundamentals: for freshers, questions are scoped to component-level
  design (e.g., design a URL shortener, design a rate limiter). Candidates are not
  expected to address full distributed-systems concerns.
- Database concepts: normalization, indexing, transaction isolation, and one SQL
  query-writing question.
- One additional data structures or algorithms problem, typically medium difficulty.

### Stage 4 — HR Round (20–30 minutes)
Conducted by an HR business partner. Topics include career goals, relocation readiness
(all new hires join Pune or Hyderabad for the first 18 months), compensation discussion,
and offer acceptance timeline. Candidates who do not receive an offer after Stage 3 are
informed before the HR round.

## Important Notes

- All four stages must be cleared in sequence on the same day. No candidate is carried
  forward to a future drive.
- Offer letters are issued within 10 business days of the drive date.
- NovaTech has a zero-tolerance policy for use of AI-assistance tools during the online
  assessment; violations result in permanent disqualification from all future drives.
