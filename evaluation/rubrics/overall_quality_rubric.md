# Overall Quality Rubric

This rubric evaluates the overall quality, helpfulness, and conversational coherence of the system's response for a given multi-turn interaction.

Score 1-5 based on the following anchors:

- **1 (Poor)**: Response is completely unhelpful, hallucinates facts, or completely loses track of the conversational history.
- **2 (Fair)**: Response attempts to answer but is disjointed, ignores previous context, or fails to extract/handle the user's intent properly.
- **3 (Good)**: Response is generally accurate and helpful. It maintains conversation history adequately but may be slightly generic or lack polish.
- **4 (Very Good)**: Response is highly coherent, maintains perfect context across turns, and fully satisfies the user's request efficiently.
- **5 (Excellent)**: Response is exceptionally natural, perfectly contextualized, and provides a seamless, highly supportive user experience.
