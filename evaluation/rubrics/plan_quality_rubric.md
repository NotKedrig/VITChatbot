# Plan Quality Rubric

This rubric evaluates the quality of the study plan generated by the LLM system. The plan must be structured and directly address the user's specific context.

Score 1-5 based on the following anchors:

- **1 (Poor)**: No plan generated, or plan is completely unstructured/unrelated to the user's request. Ignores weak topics entirely.
- **2 (Fair)**: A generic plan is provided. It lacks specificity or fails to adequately prioritize the student's reported weak topics.
- **3 (Good)**: A structured plan is provided. It addresses the user's request and makes an attempt to incorporate the target companies and skill profile, though it may lack detail or depth.
- **4 (Very Good)**: A highly structured and detailed plan. It effectively prioritizes weak topics and perfectly aligns with the requested timeline/target companies.
- **5 (Excellent)**: An outstanding, personalized, and actionable plan. It not only addresses all constraints perfectly but also provides insightful strategic recommendations for mastering the weak topics.
